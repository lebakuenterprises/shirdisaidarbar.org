# Shirdi Sai Darbar

The website of Shirdi Sai Darbar, a volunteer-run Shirdi Sai Baba temple in Sunnyvale, California: where devotees find today's timings, upcoming happenings, live darshan and ways to give.

## The temple

**Darbar**:
The temple itself, Shirdi Sai Darbar at 255 San Geronimo Way, Sunnyvale.
_Avoid_: mandir (in UI copy, except in the intro line), site, location

**Murti**:
The consecrated statue of Shri Sai Baba in the Darbar.
_Avoid_: idol, statue, deity image

**Darshan**:
Seeing Baba at the murti, in person or through **Live darshan**, the Twitch stream that runs during Aartis.
_Avoid_: livestream, video

## Time

**Aarti**:
One of the four daily services, always named Kakad, Madhyan, Dhoop and Shej.
_Avoid_: prayer, puja (for these four), service

**Schedule**:
The recurring pattern of Aartis and temple hours, the same every week.
_Avoid_: timings table, calendar

**Event**:
A dated, one-off happening at the Darbar posted by an admin, which disappears after its end date. A happening that recurs (for example the monthly Satyanarayana Puja) is one Event per occurrence.
_Avoid_: announcement, programme, recurring event

**Highlight**:
A time-limited slide the admin posts to promote one thing (an Event, a campaign, an update, a photo). It points at that thing and is never the only place its details live.
_Avoid_: banner, slide (alone), announcement

## Giving

**Seva**:
A standing, named offering a devotee sponsors, such as Annadanam, Baba's robe or sponsoring an Aarti.
_Avoid_: donation option, package

**Donation**:
A gift with no Seva attached, to the general fund or to the Temple construction.
_Avoid_: contribution, offering (as a noun for money)

**Sponsorship**:
Giving tied to one Event, made through that Event's own link (usually Paybee).
_Avoid_: festival seva, campaign

**Temple construction**:
The project to build the new marble temple: a $3M immediate goal toward a $10M full vision.
_Avoid_: new temple fund, building fund

**Priest service**:
A puja the priest performs on a devotee's request, arranged by contacting the Darbar rather than through giving.
_Avoid_: paid seva, priest booking
