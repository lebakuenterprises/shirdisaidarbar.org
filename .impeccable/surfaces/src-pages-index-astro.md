## Direction contract

THESIS: The page tells time the way the Darbar does, by its four aartis. It refuses the category default of a photo hero over a fact strip and a stack of equal bands.

OWN-WORLD: Sannidhi as specified: kafni ivory and sandal bands, sindoor maroon, bhagwa saffron, mukut gold; Tiro display, Anek text; toran twice, dhuni flame on every heading, Dwarkamai arches on frames, kolam on sandal bands; in Day no dark section: the DS Sannidhi hero is an ivory sanctum by day and the dark sanctum by night; below it two grounds only, gold-sandal and ivory, strictly alternating (M2.6), the kolam on the hero's open ground, Events and footer; lamp light only on what is now or next, everything else at rest (raise from the bioluminescent-wake challenger: light marks only the active).

STORY: In one glance a devotee knows where the day is and what comes next, sees Baba, and can go, watch or give. Each fact is stated once; neighbouring bands always differ in ground.
